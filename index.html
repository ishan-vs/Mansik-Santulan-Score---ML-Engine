          About

          About Mansik Santulan Score

          What this is

          Mansik Santulan Score – ML Engine is a student wellness project. You enter 12 pieces of information about your profile, digital habits, lifestyle and perceived stress, and a hosted machine-learning service returns a numeric predicted_mental_health_score, displayed here on a 0–10 scale.

          Everything else on this page — mood tracking, charts, badges, exercises and suggestions — runs in your browser using simple, transparent logic.

          Intended use

          Self-reflection and demonstration of a machine-learning workflow. It is meant to prompt curiosity about habits, not to evaluate anyone.

          Not a diagnosis

          The score is a statistical estimate. It cannot diagnose, detect or rule out any mental-health condition, and it should never replace talking with a qualified professional. If you are struggling, please reach out to someone you trust or a mental-health professional, or contact local emergency services if you are in danger.

          Known limitations

          • The score depends entirely on the 12 answers entered; self-reported values can be inaccurate.
          • Any single score is a snapshot. Differences between two runs are not necessarily meaningful.
          • Suggestions are rule-of-thumb, general wellness ideas, not model explanations and not medical advice.
          • History, mood entries and journal text are saved in this browser's local storage — specific to this device and browser, not encrypted, and lost if you clear site data.

          Settings & privacy

          Where your data lives. Assessment history, mood check-ins, journal text, badges and session counts are saved in this browser's localStorage. It is specific to this device and browser, it is not encrypted, and anyone with access to this browser profile could read it. It is not equivalent to secure server-side storage.

          What leaves your device. Only the 12 assessment answers are sent to the prediction service when you submit the assessment form. Journal entries are never sent anywhere.

          Browser storage appears to be unavailable, so nothing will be saved between visits.
